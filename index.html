Our roadmap

From Letters to Confident Learning

Each stage will have its own dedicated pages and activities.

🌱 Beginning
Letters → Sounds → Words → Sentences Capital Letters, Small Letters, Jolly Phonics, picture words, word building and early reading.
🌿 Elementary
Words → Reading → Grammar → Writing Vocabulary, word families, blending, basic grammar, reading and short writing.
🌳 Middle
Understanding → Expression Comprehension, grammar in context, speaking, vocabulary and creative writing.
🚀 Advanced
Independent & Confident Learning Advanced reading, writing, communication, critical thinking and creative projects.
