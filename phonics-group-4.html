🎵 Jolly Phonics Group 4

ai, j, oa, ie, ee, or

These sounds help us read many more words!

🔤 Meet the Sounds

ai

/ai/

rain • train • tail

j

/j/

jam • jet • jug

oa

/oa/

boat • goat • road

ie

/ie/

tie • pie • lie

ee

/ee/

see • tree • feet

or

/or/

fork • corn • storm

🔊 Blend & Read

r + ai + n → rain
j + a + m → jam
g + oa + t → goat
t + ie → tie
f + ee + t → feet
f + or + k → fork

📖 Short Story

The Goat and the Boat

A goat is near a boat.
The boat is on the road.
It starts to rain.
The goat sees a tree.
The goat runs to the tree.
