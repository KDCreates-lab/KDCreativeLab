🔊 Blend the Sounds

g + u + m → gum
b + u + n → bun
f + o + g → fog
l + o + g → log
b + a + g → bag

📖 Short Story

The Big Bug

A big bug is on a log.
The bug is red.
A boy has a bag.
The boy looks at the bug.
The bug can run.

✏️ Practice

1. Circle the word that starts with /b/.

bag    fog    log

2. Complete the word:

b + u + __ = bun

3. Read aloud:

bag – bug – log – fog – bun – gum

🎯 Group 3 Goal

I can recognise the Group 3 sounds, blend sounds together, read simple words and understand short sentences.
