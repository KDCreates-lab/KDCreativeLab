Start your English learning journey from the very beginning. Learn letters, sounds, words, sentences and reading step by step.

🔡

Small Letters

Learn a–z small letters with pictures, sounds, tracing and practice.

Capital & Small Matching

Match capital letters with their corresponding small letters.

2

🔊

Letter Sounds

Learn the sounds of letters and practise hearing them.

👂

Beginning Sounds

Identify the beginning sound of simple words.

3

📝 First Words

📚

First Words

Learn simple words using familiar letters and sounds.

🧩

CVC Words

Build and read simple consonant-vowel-consonant words.

4

✏️ Simple Sentences

💬

Simple Sentences

Start reading and understanding short simple sentences.

5

📖 First Reading

📖

First Reading

Read short, easy passages using the sounds and words you have learned.

🎯 Beginner Goal

By completing this learning path, children will gradually move from recognising letters and sounds to reading simple words, sentences and short passages.
