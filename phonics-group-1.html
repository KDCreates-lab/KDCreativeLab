🔤 Jolly Phonics Group 1

Learn the first six sounds and use them to build words, blend sounds and begin reading.

🌱 Meet Group 1

In Group 1 we learn these six sounds:

s • a • t • i • p • n

Once we know these sounds, we can start blending them to read simple words.

📖 First Reading

Read the words slowly. Blend the sounds if needed.

sat    sit    tap    tip
pin    pan    nap    pat
at    an    as    pit

📚 First Sentences

Read only after the child is comfortable blending the words.

I sit.
I nap.
I tap.
I sit at a pit.

Teacher note: As new phonics groups are introduced, we will gradually increase the number of words and sentences the child can read.

🌟 Group 1 Goal

By the end of Group 1, the child should be able to:

✔ Recognise s, a, t, i, p, n
✔ Say their sounds
✔ Blend simple sounds
✔ Read simple Group 1 words
✔ Build words using learned letters
✔ Begin reading simple sentences
