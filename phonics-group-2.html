📚 Read Simple Sentences

Read the words slowly. Then read the whole sentence.

🎵 Sound Practice Poem

H says /h/, h says /h/,

H is for hat.

R says /r/, r says /r/,

R is for rat.

D says /d/, d says /d/,

D is for dog.

Listen, say, blend and read! 🌱

📖 Little Sound Story

🐱 The Cat and the Red Hen

A cat sat on a mat. A red hen sat near the cat.

The hen had a hat. The cat had a nap.

The hen ran. The cat ran too.

They had fun!

Try: Point to the words you can read.
