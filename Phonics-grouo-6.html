f + o + x → fox
b + o + x → box
ch + i + p → chip
sh + i + p → ship
sh + o + p → shop
b + a + th → bath

📖 Short Story

The Fox and the Fish

A fox is near a shop.
The fox sees a fish.
The fish is in a box.
The fox looks at the box.
Then the fox runs away.
