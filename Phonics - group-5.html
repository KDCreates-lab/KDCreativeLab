🔊 Blend & Read

z + i + p → zip
w + e + t → wet
r + i + ng → ring
s + i + ng → sing
v + a + n → van

📖 Short Story

The King and the Ring

A king has a ring.
The ring is in a box.
The king looks for the ring.
He finds it in the box.
The king is happy.
